# Legal & Compliance Memorandum: Charitable Donation Tax Receipts
**Entity**: Drodul Phendey Ling Foundation (Gelephu, Sarpang Dzongkhag, Kingdom of Bhutan)  
**Date**: September 2026  
**Subject**: Legal Basis for Issuing Donation Receipts & Tax Exemption Compliance (Bhutan Domestic vs. India 80G vs. International)

---

## 1. Background & Entity Jurisdiction
1. **Jurisdiction of Incorporation**: Drodul Phendey Ling Foundation is an ecclesiastical and charitable institution established in Gelephu, Sarpang Dzongkhag, within the sovereign Kingdom of Bhutan, registered under the Religious Organizations Act / Civil Society Organizations Act of Bhutan.
2. **Tax Status in Bhutan**:
   - Under Chapter 2 of the **Income Tax Act of the Kingdom of Bhutan (2001)**, religious and charitable organizations are recognized as exempt entities.
   - Business donations made by corporate or individual taxpayers within Bhutan to registered religious organizations are allowable deductions subject to the Ministry of Finance regulations.
   - Receipts issued for domestic Bhutanese donors cite the official Foundation Registration and Tax Exemption Approval number (`DPL/TAX-EXEMPT/BTN/2026/092`).

---

## 2. Indian Income Tax Act (Section 80G) Compliance Assessment
1. **Section 80G Qualification**:
   - Under Section 80G(2) and Section 12AA/12AB of the Indian Income Tax Act (1961), tax deductions are available to Indian taxpayers only for donations made to institutions or funds established in India and approved by the Principal Commissioner or Commissioner of Income Tax.
   - A foreign-incorporated entity (including Bhutanese organizations) **cannot directly issue Section 80G certificates** to Indian donors unless:
     a) The donation is received and receipted by a duly registered Indian sister trust/society with valid 12AB/80G registrations and FCRA compliance, OR
     b) A bilateral tax treaty or specific central government notification applies.
2. **Legal Risk Mitigation**:
   - Blanket hardcoding of "80G" on all automated receipts issued to international/Indian donors without routing through an Indian approved entity creates non-compliance risk.
   - **Remediation Action**: Universal receipts are refactored to **"Official Charitable Donation Receipt"**. If an Indian donor contributes via an affiliated Indian partner trust, the Indian partner's 80G registration is conditionally applied; otherwise, standard official donation receipts are provided.

---

## 3. Cross-Border Donations & FCRA Considerations
1. **Cross-Border Remittances**:
   - Donations received from foreign nationals (outside Bhutan and India) are acknowledged with official international charitable donation receipts stating the purpose, amount, transaction reference, and Foundation registration.
   - Donors are advised to consult their local tax jurisdictions (e.g., US 501(c)(3) equivalency or UK Gift Aid) regarding cross-border deductibility.

---

- **Universal Title**: `Official Charitable Donation Receipt` (replacing hardcoded `80G Money Receipt`).

  - Indian Partner Trust Donations: `Indian 80G Reg: Approved under Sec 80G(5) of IT Act, 1961 (Conditional on Partner Entity)`.
  - International Donations: `Registered Buddhist Charitable Institution (Kingdom of Bhutan)`.
